🧠 Core Blockchain Concept: Why Store Only Hashes On-Chain?
Beginners often ask: "Why don't we upload the entire photo or design directly into the smart contract?"
There are two fundamental blockchain engineering reasons:
  • Gas Fees & Storage Scalability: Storing 1 MB of raw binary data on a blockchain is prohibitively expensive. A 64-character SHA-256 hash costs fractions of a cent in transaction fees.
  • Confidentiality & Privacy: All data written to a public blockchain is permanently visible to anyone in the world. Storing only the SHA-256 hash creates mathematically irrevocable proof of possession without revealing a single pixel of your proprietary creative work.
