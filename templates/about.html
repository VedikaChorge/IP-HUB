About IP Evidence Hub

A Blockchain-Based Platform for Intellectual Property Evidence Management & Proof-of-Existence.

🎓
Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) • First-Year B.Sc. Blockchain Student Project

Developed for presentation to MST Blockchain as an applied technical demonstration of cryptographic hashing, steganography, and MST Testnet smart contract notarization for digital intellectual property protection.

⚠️ 1. The Problem: The Fragility of Digital Evidence

In today's digital economy, independent creators, UI/UX designers, photographers, developers, and small businesses face a daunting challenge: digital files can be copied, modified, and redistributed in milliseconds without attribution.

When an intellectual property dispute arises, traditional operating system file creation dates are trivially manipulated, and proving who possessed the original creation first often turns into an expensive, chaotic battle of assertions. Creators rarely maintain organized, time-stamped, tamper-evident records of their preliminary work, native project layers, and drafts.

💡 2. The Solution: IP Evidence Hub

IP Evidence Hub bridges the gap between creative workflows and cryptographic legal preparedness. By providing an intuitive, full-stack management platform, users can:

🔒 Cryptographic Hashing:

Computes mathematically irreversible SHA-256 digital fingerprints of exact original file bytes.

🛡️ LSB Invisible Watermarking:

Embeds traceable identifiers into lossless pixel channels to assist in tracing derivative copies.

📋 Smart Evidence Gap Checker:

Evaluates supporting documentation (drafts, commits, contracts) across a 7-point audit checklist.

📑 Evidence Pack Generator:

Produces professional, court-ready PDF evidence packages via ReportLab with embedded QR verification.

🔐 Controlled Share Rooms:

Shares verified proof-of-existence records with clients or lawyers via revocable tokens.

⛓️ MST Testnet Notarization:

Anchors hashes permanently to a decentralized smart contract on MST Testnet without leaking confidential data.

⛓️ 3. Blockchain's Precise Role: Decentralized Proof-of-Existence

A centralized database can theoretically be altered by its administrator. By committing the SHA-256 file hash and Evidence ID to an EVM-compatible smart contract on MST Testnet (EvidenceRegistry.sol), the existence of that exact file at that exact block timestamp becomes mathematically unalterable and globally verifiable by any third party without needing to trust our servers.

Privacy Guarantee: The actual file, images, and creator personal details never leave our secure server. Only the 32-byte cryptographic digest is recorded on-chain.
💼 4. Potential Business Models (Commercial Viability)

Free monthly vault registrations for students and hobbyists with local database verification.

Professional Subscription:

Automated on-chain gas subsidization, bulk PDF generation, and priority watermark scanning for studios.

Enterprise IP Escrow:

API integration for GitHub repos, Figma plugins, and design agency client handover workflows.

⚖️ Critical Legal Reality: IP Evidence Hub is an engineering tool for digital evidence gathering and forensic timestamping. Registering a creation in this system establishes technical proof of possession at a specific point in time. It does NOT automatically confer statutory legal copyright ownership, which is established under national legal frameworks (such as the Indian Copyright Act, 1957) and adjudicated in judicial courts.
